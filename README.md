I am a scientific computing researcher based in London, working where numerical
methods meet quantitative finance and machine learning. I hold an MPhil in
Scientific Computing from the University of Cambridge, where I worked on
massively parallel sparse linear solvers under Prof. Hrvoje Jasak, and an MSc in
Artificial Intelligence from Queen's University Belfast. The repositories here
are small, self-contained studies: a GPU-accelerated finite-volume solver for the
compressible Euler equations, ruin probabilities under the Cramér–Lundberg model,
discrete delta hedging under Black–Scholes, and learning-based autofocus for
microscope image stacks. Each one pairs the code with a short write-up, and
wherever a result can be obtained two ways, it is: simulation against a closed
form, GPU against CPU, one estimator against another.
